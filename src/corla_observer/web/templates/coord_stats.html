Observation statistics

As of event {{ snap.head_seq }}; this page reloads itself every 15 seconds. How it counts: Observers enter each ballot with two taps or contest by contest, as each chooses. With two taps, a match takes one tap on the first reading of a ballot and one on the second. Contest by contest, each contest is confirmed, heard instead or not heard clearly, in each reading; the ballot counts as entered once it is marked done or every contest has an outcome in the second reading, as a match if every contest was confirmed. Entry time is from a ballot appearing on the observer's screen to their entry (for a match, the second tap); time to first reading is from the ballot appearing to the first tap, and time to confirmation from the first tap to the second. Edits are not timed.
