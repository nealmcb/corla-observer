Which of {{ county }}'s own (local) contests random draws from their cards would help most. Local contests carried by exactly the same cards form one sampling domain: a draw from it counts for every one of them. Each contest's pooled risk is Kaplan-Markov over CORLA's draws that landed on its cards, counted with multiplicity, plus the domain draws made here, with the discrepancies seen on them; risk limit {{ limit }}. Extra draws are assumed to show no discrepancy until examined. Draws are with replacement: a card drawn twice is pulled once and counted twice.

Each contest is thin (pooled risk above {{ limit }}) or settled, and has any of these tags: {{ badge('info', 'unseen') }} no card of the official sample carried it (domain draws are shown apart: “seen by N domain draws”); {{ badge('high', 'flagged') }} a discrepancy was found on its cards, with its types; {{ badge('tag', 'uncontested') }} no more candidates than seats, so no risk; {{ badge('tag', 'recount') }} a margin of at most 0.5% of the highest vote, or for a multi-winner contest of the lowest winner's votes (C.R.S. 1-10.5-101), for information only: Colorado's recounts usually re-tabulate by machine, so it is checked like any other; {{ badge('tag', 'tie') }} a margin of zero: each card checks the CVR's count, but the risk can't fall.
