Each county's extract: its selected ballots in pull order with their expected votes, from its pull list and redacted CVR (or CORLA's posted selections, or until then the predicted selections, marked as such). Print the PDF at actual size (100%).

More about the extracts and their printouts

Every printed page carries one short identification line (county, board, page N of M, and the first 8 hex digits of the extract's hash and of the page's hash) and a QR code of the page's hash. Each board's print starts with its provenance page, which lists the seed, the extract's hash, every input's hash and the page index's hash in full (each page's hash is in the CSV). Names are shortened to fit their columns, never cut to share a line: a contest's several choices take a line each; the provenance page gives the key to the abbreviations. The printout is a PDF, the same on every printer and phone and the same bytes whenever it is drawn (its hash is recorded with the extract): one page per sheet, in one of four layouts (portrait with one or two columns of ballots, landscape with two or three).
